POPULAR RECIPES

Some of the recipes that you can find in this page.

Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Cook spaghetti and sauté with garlic, olive oil, and chili flakes.

Chicken Stir-Fry

Sauté chicken with vegetables and soy sauce for a quick meal.

Tacos al Pastor

Marinate pork, grill, and serve in corn tortillas with pineapple and onions.

Vegetable Curry

Cook assorted vegetables with coconut milk and curry spices.

Pancakes

Mix flour, milk, and eggs, then cook on a skillet until golden brown.

Beef Stroganoff

Sauté beef strips and mushrooms, then simmer in a creamy sauce.

Caesar Salad

Toss romaine lettuce with Caesar dressing, croutons, and parmesan.

Minestrone Soup

Simmer vegetables, beans, and pasta in vegetable broth.

Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Melt cheese between two slices of buttered bread on a skillet.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Mix dough with chocolate chips and bake until golden.

Quiche Lorraine

Bake a pie crust filled with eggs, cream, cheese, and bacon.

Shrimp Scampi

Sauté shrimp with garlic, butter, and lemon juice, served over pasta.

Stuffed Bell Peppers

Fill bell peppers with a mixture of rice, meat, and spices, then bake.

Egg Fried Rice

Stir-fry cooked rice with scrambled eggs, peas, and soy sauce for a quick and satisfying dish.
